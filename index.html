Make worksheets — it’s free

Eight ways to practice the same words

  • Flash cards: 8 to a sheet, printed double-sided.
  • Study sheet: word study, fill in the blank with a word bank, and matching.
  • Quiz: multiple choice and matching, up to four shuffled versions.
  • Bingo and word wall: unique cards, a caller sheet and cut-out word wall cards.
  • Word search: easy, medium or challenge.
  • Crossword: clues from your definitions, with letter counts.
  • Frayer model: definition, picture, examples and non-examples.
  • Class glossary: a searchable A–Z page your class can add to and edit.

Three steps

  1. Add your words: one per line, a dash, then the meaning. Or upload a CSV, or pick a sample list.
  2. Pick a worksheet and see it exactly as it will print.
  3. Print it or save it as a PDF, on US Letter or A4.
