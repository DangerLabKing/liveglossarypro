How to make vocabulary bingo cards

By Cliff Wright · November 3, 2026

Vocabulary bingo turns review into a game. You read a meaning aloud, and students find and cover the matching word on their card. Live Glossary Pro prints the cards from your own word list for free, with no sign-up.

What you get

  • Bingo cards. Choose how many, from 1 to 40. Every card is different, and each square shows the word with a small picture or a colored letter tile.
  • A caller sheet. Every word and its meaning in a shuffled list, with a box to tick so you know which ones you have called.
  • A word wall. The words and meanings as cards to cut out and display.

How many words you need

Bingo needs at least 8 words. The card size changes with your list:

  • 8 to 15 words: 3 by 3 cards with a free space in the middle
  • 16 to 23 words: 4 by 4 cards
  • 24 or more words: full 5 by 5 cards with B-I-N-G-O across the top and a free space

Example: an Earth and space unit

These eight words make 3 by 3 cards. Copy them into Live Glossary Pro:

planet — a large body in space that orbits a star

star — a giant ball of hot gas that gives off its own light

orbit — the curved path one object takes around another in space

rotation — one full spin of an object on its axis

revolution — one full trip of an object around another, such as Earth around the Sun

gravity — the force that pulls objects toward each other

axis — an imaginary line that an object spins around

constellation — a group of stars that forms a pattern in the night sky

Because you read the meaning and students look for the word, every call is a small vocabulary check.

Quick answers

How many bingo cards can I print?

You can print from 1 to 40 cards, and every card is different.

How many words do I need for vocabulary bingo?

At least 8 words. With 24 or more you get full 5 by 5 cards with a free space.
