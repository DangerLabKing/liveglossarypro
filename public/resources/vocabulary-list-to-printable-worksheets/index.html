How to turn a vocabulary list into printable worksheets

By Cliff Wright · November 3, 2026

You can turn a vocabulary list into printable worksheets in a few minutes with Live Glossary Pro. It is free for teachers, and you do not need to sign up or create an account.

How it works

Type or paste your words, one per line, with a dash between each word and its meaning. You can also upload a CSV. Then pick a worksheet: flash cards, a study sheet, a quiz, bingo, a word search, a crossword, Frayer models or a class glossary. The preview shows exactly what will print, and each worksheet prints on paper or saves as a PDF.

Example: an ecosystems unit

Here are four terms from a science unit on ecosystems. You can copy them straight into Live Glossary Pro.

habitat — the natural place where an organism lives, such as a forest or a pond

producer — an organism that makes its own food, such as a plant

consumer — an organism that gets energy by eating other organisms, such as a rabbit or a hawk

decomposer — an organism that breaks down dead plants and animals and returns nutrients to the soil, such as a mushroom

Which worksheets to print

Flash cards work well for a quick warm-up, with students quizzing each other in pairs.

A Frayer model is a word map with four boxes around each word. The definition is filled in for you. Students rewrite it in their own words, draw a picture, and list examples and non-examples.

The quiz has two parts. In Part A, students circle the best answer to multiple-choice questions. In Part B, they match each word to its meaning. You can print up to four versions with the same questions in a shuffled order, so students sitting near each other get different layouts.

Because every worksheet comes from the same list, you can use flash cards early in the week and the quiz at the end without retyping anything. Save the list as a CSV and you can load it again next year.
