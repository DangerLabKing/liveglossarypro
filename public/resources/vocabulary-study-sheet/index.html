How to make a vocabulary study sheet

By Cliff Wright · November 3, 2026

A study sheet gives students three kinds of practice with the same words, on one printable worksheet. Live Glossary Pro builds it from your word list for free, with no sign-up, and adds an answer key.

The three parts

  • Part A, Word Study. Every word with its meaning and a picture. Students read each word and say it out loud.
  • Part B, Fill in the Blank. Students read each meaning and write the word it describes, using the Word Bank on the page.
  • Part C, Matching. Students draw a line from each word to its meaning, or write the letter on the blank. This part appears when your list has at least 3 words.

The answer key for Parts B and C starts on a new page at the end, so you can leave it out when you print copies for students.

Example: a civics unit

Copy these six words into Live Glossary Pro and choose Study sheet:

government — the group of people who make and carry out the laws for a community, state or country

citizen — a member of a country who has rights and responsibilities

constitution — a written plan that sets up a government and its basic laws

democracy — a government in which the people hold the power, often by voting

vote — to make a choice in an election

law — a rule that people in a community must follow

When to use it

The study sheet works well as an introduction to a new unit or as homework, since Part A gives students the meanings before they practice. Later in the unit, a quiz from the same list checks what they remember.

Quick answers

What is on a vocabulary study sheet?

Word study with meanings and pictures, fill in the blank with a word bank, matching, and an answer key.

Is the answer key on a separate page?

Yes. The answer key always starts on its own page at the end.
