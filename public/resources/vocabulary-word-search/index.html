How to make a vocabulary word search

By Cliff Wright · November 3, 2026

Live Glossary Pro turns your vocabulary list into a printable word search, with an answer key. You pick the difficulty, so the same list works for different grades. It is free for teachers, and there is no sign-up.

Three difficulty levels

  • Easy: words run across and down only, with a smaller grid and bigger letters. Up to 10 words.
  • Medium: adds diagonals. Up to 14 words.
  • Challenge: adds words written backwards in every direction. Up to 18 words.

If your list has more words than one grid holds, it is split into up to four puzzles instead of dropping words. Any word that does not fit is listed on the answer key.

Example: a Thanksgiving word search

These eight words fit in a single Easy puzzle. Copy them into Live Glossary Pro and choose Word search:

harvest — the gathering of crops when they are ripe

gratitude — a feeling of being thankful

feast — a large, special meal shared with others

cornucopia — a horn-shaped basket filled with fruits and vegetables, a symbol of plenty

autumn — the season between summer and winter, also called fall

tradition — a custom passed down from one generation to the next

gather — to come together in one place

thankful — feeling glad about something good

Students circle each word in the grid and tick it off the list on the page.

Pair it with the meanings

A word search is good practice for spelling and recognizing words, but it does not use the meanings. Because Live Glossary Pro keeps your list, you can print flash cards or a quiz from the same words for the meanings, without retyping anything.

Quick answers

Does the word search come with an answer key?

Yes. Every puzzle has an answer key.

What do the difficulty levels change?

Easy uses across and down only, Medium adds diagonals, and Challenge adds backwards words in every direction.
