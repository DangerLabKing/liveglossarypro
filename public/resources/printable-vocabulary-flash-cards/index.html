How to make printable vocabulary flash cards

By Cliff Wright · November 3, 2026

Live Glossary Pro prints vocabulary flash cards from your own word list, 8 cards to a sheet. The word and a picture go on the front, and the meaning goes on the back. It is free for teachers, with no sign-up.

How the double-sided printing works

The cards print as pairs of pages: a front sheet, then a back sheet. The back sheet is laid out as a mirror image, so when you print double-sided, each meaning lands right behind its word. Every card is numbered on both sides, which makes it easy to check.

  1. Choose Flash cards and click Print / Save as PDF.
  2. In the print window, turn on double-sided printing and choose flip on long edge.
  3. Set Scale to 100%.
  4. Cut along the dashed lines.

If your printer cannot print double-sided, print the fronts and backs on separate sheets and glue them together before cutting.

Example: figurative language

These eight terms fill exactly one sheet of cards. Copy them into Live Glossary Pro:

simile — a comparison using "like" or "as," such as "fast as a cheetah"

metaphor — a comparison that says one thing is another, such as "the classroom was a zoo"

personification — giving human qualities to something that is not human, such as "the wind whispered"

hyperbole — an exaggeration for effect, such as "I have a million things to do"

alliteration — repeating the same first sound in nearby words, such as "Sally sells seashells"

onomatopoeia — a word that sounds like the noise it names, such as "buzz" or "crash"

idiom — a phrase whose meaning is different from its words, such as "break a leg"

imagery — language that appeals to the five senses

Ways to use them

Students can quiz each other in pairs, sort the cards into groups, or take a set home to study. Because the cards come from the same list as your other worksheets, you can print a quiz from the same words at the end of the week.

Quick answers

How many flash cards fit on a page?

Eight cards fit on each sheet, with the fronts and backs on separate pages.

How do I print vocabulary flash cards double-sided?

Print double-sided and choose flip on long edge. The back sheet is mirrored so each meaning lines up behind its word.
