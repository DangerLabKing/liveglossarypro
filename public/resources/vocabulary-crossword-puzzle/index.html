How to make a vocabulary crossword

By Cliff Wright · November 3, 2026

Live Glossary Pro builds a printable crossword from your vocabulary list. Your meanings become the clues, and an answer key is included. It is free for teachers, with no sign-up.

How the clues work

Each clue is the meaning you typed for that word, listed under Across or Down. If the word itself appears in its own meaning, it is hidden so the clue does not give the answer away. A number in brackets after each clue shows how many letters the answer has, and two-word terms show both lengths, such as (4, 5).

Example: a properties of matter unit

Copy these six words into Live Glossary Pro and choose Crossword:

solid — matter that has a definite shape and volume

liquid — matter that has a definite volume but takes the shape of its container

gas — matter that has no definite shape or volume

mass — the amount of matter in an object

volume — the amount of space an object takes up

density — how much mass is packed into a certain volume

Longer lists

Words need 3 to 19 letters to fit, and you need at least 2. Each crossword holds up to 20 words. A longer list is split into up to four crosswords instead of dropping words, and any word that cannot fit is listed on the answer key, so nothing disappears without you knowing.

Quick answers

Does the crossword come with an answer key?

Yes. The answer key lists every Across and Down answer, plus any words that were too long to fit.

How many words can one crossword hold?

Up to 20. Longer lists are split into up to four crosswords.
