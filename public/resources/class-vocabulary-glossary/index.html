How to make a class vocabulary glossary

By Cliff Wright · November 3, 2026

A class glossary is a searchable A to Z list of your unit's vocabulary. Live Glossary Pro builds one from your word list as a file you open in any web browser, and your class can keep adding words to it. It is free for teachers, with no sign-up.

What it does

Unlike the other formats, the class glossary is not a worksheet. It is an interactive page:

  • Words are sorted A to Z under letter headings, with a bar to jump to any letter.
  • Each word shows its meaning and a picture or a colored letter tile.
  • A search box finds words in both the words and the meanings.
  • You can add, edit and delete words.

The file opens in any browser and does not need the internet. Your changes are kept in that browser. To keep a copy with your changes, or to share it, click Save updated glossary. Export CSV saves the list as a spreadsheet you can upload to Live Glossary Pro again, and Copy list copies it as text.

Example: a water cycle unit

Start with these words, then add new ones as they come up in class. Copy them into Live Glossary Pro, choose Class glossary, and click Download class glossary file:

evaporation — when liquid water heats up and changes into water vapor

condensation — when water vapor cools and changes into liquid water

precipitation — water that falls from clouds as rain, snow, sleet or hail

runoff — water that flows over the ground into streams, rivers and lakes

groundwater — water stored underground in soil and rock

water cycle — the continuous movement of water between Earth's surface and the air

Ways to use it

Put the glossary on the board at the start of a unit and add each new word together as you teach it. By the end of the unit, the class has built its own word list. Export it as a CSV and you can print flash cards or a quiz from the same words. You can also print the glossary itself. The buttons and search box are left off the printed page.

Quick answers

Does the class glossary need the internet?

No. It is a file that opens in any web browser and works without the internet.

Can students add words to the class glossary?

Yes. You can add, edit and delete words, then click Save updated glossary to keep a copy with the changes.
